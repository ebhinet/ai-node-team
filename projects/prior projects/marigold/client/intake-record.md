## Project Overview
A complete website for Marigold Wellness Studio, a boutique yoga, Pilates, and sound bath studio in Millbrook owned by Sara Okonkwo. The site replaces an outdated, non-mobile-friendly site and prioritizes online booking as the primary user action. The website will be delivered as a runnable localhost build (HTML/CSS/JS) with placeholder integrations. Sub-deliverables — a brand design report and a site architecture & copy report — are delivered first for client alignment before the website is built.

## Scope Boundaries

**In scope:**
- Homepage with primary "Book a Class" CTA
- About / Our Story page (studio background, Sara's story, instructor bios — placeholder content in proper style)
- Class schedule page (what, when, who's teaching)
- Online booking page (placeholder embed — Pike13 or Mindbody TBD)
- Gift cards page (placeholder purchase flow)
- Corporate wellness page (chair yoga, guided breathwork — slightly more professional tone)
- Pricing / packages page
- Testimonials section (placeholder quotes in proper style)
- Contact form
- Email capture (pop-up or inline)
- Logo design (Sara has no current logo)
- Full brand identity (colors, typography, imagery direction)
- Mobile-responsive design
- Stock images/videos throughout (Sara's photos not yet available)
- Placeholder content for bios, testimonials, pricing — written in proper style for Sara to polish later

**Out of scope:**
- Blog
- Live integrations (booking system, gift card payments, email service)
- Sara's actual photography
- Paid advertising or SEO campaign
- Content management system / admin backend
- Hosting or deployment

---

## Constraints and Requirements
- **No integrations:** Booking, gift cards, and email capture use visible placeholders — no live connections
- **Stock imagery:** Sara's photos are still being processed; use appropriate stock images/videos
- **Placeholder content:** Instructor bios, testimonials, and pricing use realistic fake content in proper style
- **Design direction:** Warm, calm, grounded, distinctive. Not clinical, not trendy (no generic sage-green/beige), not corporate. Inspiration: Othership (elevated but warm), earthy/handcrafted ceramics studio feel. Grounded typography — nothing thin or wispy
- **Design anti-patterns:** No stock photos of women in white rooms smiling at cameras. No busy layouts. Nothing cold or corporate
- **Audience:** Primary — women 30-55, working professionals and moms, health-conscious but not hardcore fitness. Secondary — corporate decision-makers (HR/office managers) for corporate wellness section
- **Primary CTA:** Book a class — must be unmissable
- **Report format:** Gate 1 and Gate 2 deliverables are Word documents — low-tech, easy to read, easy to give feedback on
- **Options at gates:** Gate 1 presents 2-3 brand options. Gate 2 includes optional add-ons/alternatives for Sara's input
- **Decision-maker:** Sara Okonkwo is the sole decision-maker
