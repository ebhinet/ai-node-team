## Decisions
_Significant choices made during execution — what was decided and why._

1. **Caveat for heading typeface.** Chose a genuine handwritten Google Font rather than a brush script or calligraphic font. Brush scripts risk looking like wedding invitations; calligraphic fonts risk looking medieval. Caveat reads as "a real person wrote this" which directly serves the handcrafted concept.

2. **Source Serif 4 for body typeface.** A serif body (not sans-serif) was chosen to anchor the handwritten heading and provide gravitas. Sans-serif body + handwritten heading risks feeling like a children's brand. The serif pairing keeps it adult and credible.

3. **Saffron Ink (#C47B2B) as primary brand color.** A deep saffron gold rather than a bright yellow or orange. This reads as a natural pigment / dried-marigold tone rather than a fresh-flower color. It differentiates from Option A's warm/earthy palette (which likely uses terracotta/amber tones) by leaning into craft-pigment territory.

4. **Lichen Green (#7A8B6F) deliberately pushed grey-green.** Sara's anti-pattern calls out "generic sage-green/beige." Standard wellness green was avoided. Lichen Green sits in grey-green territory — more like actual moss on stone than a paint swatch.

5. **Hand-drawn marigold logo, not geometric or abstract.** The handcrafted direction demands a logo that looks hand-made. A geometric marigold would belong in Option B; a warm-textured but clean marigold would belong in Option A. Option C's logo should look sketched.

6. **Hands as primary imagery subject.** "Handcrafted" taken literally — the imagery direction centers on hands to visually reinforce the brand concept at every touchpoint.

- Lichen Green was a deliberate choice to avoid the sage-green anti-pattern. It may read as "dull" on first pass — that is intentional.

**For arch-001 and copy-001/copy-002 (if Option C is selected at Gate 1):**

- Photography direction emphasizes close crops and texture shots. Page wireframes should include space for detail imagery, not just hero banners.
- The "parchment-space" principle (generous whitespace) means content density should be low. Copy should be concise and breathable.

**For web-001 (if Option C is selected at Gate 1):**
- Paper texture overlays are specified but should be "barely perceptible." Implementation should use low-opacity background images, not heavy filters.
- The hand-drawn illustration accents need to be implemented as SVGs or high-res PNGs to preserve line quality. Do not use icon fonts or CSS-drawn shapes.
- Caveat is on Google Fonts and can be loaded via standard embed. Source Serif 4 is also on Google Fonts.
