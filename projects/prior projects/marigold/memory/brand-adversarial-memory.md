## Decisions
_Significant choices made during execution — what was decided and why._
_Captures rationale so a re-entering agent doesn't relitigate settled questions._

1. **Shared heading typeface (DM Serif Display) between brand-001 and brand-002 treated as High for brand-002, Medium for brand-001.** Both documents use DM Serif Display. I assigned the rework burden to brand-002 because: (a) brand-001's rationale for choosing it is stronger (warm/earthy direction maps directly to DM Serif Display's character), (b) brand-002 positions itself as "elevated modern" which should have a more contemporary type identity, and (c) changing brand-001's heading face would cascade into its logo wordmark which is more tightly integrated with the overall concept.

2. **Caveat in brand-003 flagged as Medium, not High.** Caveat sits in tension with Sara's "nothing thin or wispy" constraint, but the handcrafted concept genuinely requires a handwritten face to differentiate from the other two options. Blocking this would effectively kill Option C's distinctiveness. Flagged for careful presentation at Gate 1 rather than requiring rework.

3. **CTA contrast in brand-002 flagged as Medium, not High.** The accessibility concern is real but can be resolved by specifying button text color (Charcoal Umber or reversed Warm Ivory). This is an additive fix, not a rework of the palette itself. Flagged for downstream resolution.

4. **Cross-option distinctiveness assessed as strong.** The three options occupy clearly different conceptual lanes: warm/earthy, elevated/modern, handcrafted/organic. Palettes, logo approaches, imagery directions, and typography are all meaningfully differentiated. No concern about options blurring together.

5. **brand-002 rework validated (second pass).** Instrument Serif confirmed as a strong replacement for DM Serif Display. The originator also addressed all medium and low findings from the first pass: CTA button text color is now explicitly specified for WCAG AA, and logo petal count is fixed at 9 at 40-degree intervals. The rework was clean and targeted — no new issues introduced.
