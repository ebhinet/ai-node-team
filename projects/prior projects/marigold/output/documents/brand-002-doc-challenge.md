The handling is careful and the color is light enough to avoid triggering Sara's anti-pattern at a glance, but its presence in the Gate 1 brand report may prompt Sara to question whether her sage-green concern was heard. This is not a distinctiveness concern — body faces are functional, not brand-differentiating — but worth noting that the brand's typographic identity rests primarily on Instrument Serif at display sizes. This is appropriate for an "elevated modern" direction where the heading face does the personality work.
