### Image Selection Principles (Rework)

All images in this rework were selected against these criteria from the brand-002 brief:
- **Mood:** Quiet confidence — still, composed, intentional, editorial
- **Lighting:** Natural light, soft directional, warm white balance
- **Color treatment:** Muted, warm, desaturated — no high saturation
- **Subjects:** Studio interiors with natural materials, people mid-practice with eyes closed/gaze averted, detail shots, natural materials
- **Composition:** Clean, spacious, generous negative space
- **Avoided:** Posed smiling at camera, high saturation, fitness/athletic imagery, busy compositions, dark/moody, generic beige/sage

### Image Reuse (Intentional)

- `1552196563-55cd4e45efb3` (seated meditation) — Homepage hero and classes hero. The strongest single image for representing the brand; reuse maintains cohesion.
- `1592895792095-85fa785192a9` (overhead mat meditation) — Homepage yoga card, classes yoga section, and instructors hero. Editorial overhead composition that represents the practice beautifully.
- `1593811167562-9cef47bfc4d7` (singing bowl) — Homepage sound bath card and classes sound bath section. Direct subject match.
