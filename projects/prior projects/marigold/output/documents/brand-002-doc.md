## Color Palette

### Primary Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Warm Ivory | `#F5F0E8` | A warm off-white that anchors every page. Avoids the sterility of pure white while staying clean and contemporary. Derived from natural linen tones — grounded without reading as "beige wellness cliche." |
| Lead Accent | Muted Terracotta Rose | `#C4887B` | A dusty, desaturated terracotta-pink. This is the brand's signature warmth carrier — it reads as elevated and intentional rather than earthy or boho. Used for primary CTA button backgrounds ("Book a Class"), key headings, and brand moments. **CTA button text:** use Charcoal Umber (`#3B3230`) for body-size text or Warm Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) reversed for large/bold text to meet WCAG AA contrast. Tested well against wellness anti-patterns: it is neither sage-green nor generic blush. |
| Depth | Charcoal Umber | `#3B3230` | A brown-black that replaces pure black throughout. Warmer than charcoal, more sophisticated than dark brown. Used for body text, logo, and structural elements. Provides gravitas without coldness. |
| Grounding | Warm Stone | `#B8AFA6` | A mid-tone greige (gray-beige) for secondary elements, dividers, borders, and muted backgrounds. Functions as the visual "breath" between content sections. Keeps layouts feeling spacious and curated. |

### Secondary / Accent Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highlight | Burnished Gold | `#C9A85C` | A muted, antique gold — not bright or metallic. Reserved for subtle accents: thin rules, icon highlights, hover states. Connects to the marigold flower without being literal yellow. Used sparingly to maintain its impact. |
| Soft Ground | Pale Sage | `#D5D8CE` | A barely-there sage used only in large background blocks or alternating sections. Light enough to avoid the "generic wellness sage" trap Sara flagged — it reads as neutral gray with a whisper of green, not as a color statement. |

### Palette Rationale

This palette is built on deliberate restraint. The warm ivory and charcoal umber do the structural work. The terracotta rose provides the singular, ownable color moment — distinctive enough to be recognizable, warm enough to honor Sara's vision, sophisticated enough to feel elevated above typical studio branding. The burnished gold connects to the marigold namesake through a refined, abstracted reference rather than a literal flower color. Every color was selected to avoid Sara's anti-patterns: nothing clinical, nothing trendy-generic, nothing that reads as mass-market wellness.

## Typography

### Heading Typeface: Instrument Serif

**Source:** Google Fonts (free)
**Classification:** Contemporary editorial serif

Instrument Serif is a clean, contemporary serif designed for editorial display use. Its letterforms are sharp and confident — high contrast between thick and thin strokes, crisp wedge serifs, and open counters that give it a distinctly modern, magazine-quality presence. Unlike warm/rounded serifs, Instrument Serif reads as architectural and precise, which anchors the "elevated modern" positioning of Option B. It carries enough stroke weight and structural substance to satisfy Sara's "grounded, not thin or wispy" constraint — the thick strokes provide visual heft, while the thin strokes create elegant contrast rather than fragility. At display sizes, it immediately signals refinement and editorial sophistication.

**Distinctiveness:** Instrument Serif is visually distinct from DM Serif Display (used in Option A), which has rounded terminals and softer, warmer proportions. It is equally distinct from Caveat (Option C's handwritten face). Where DM Serif Display reads as approachable and established, Instrument Serif reads as sharp and contemporary — a clear differentiation when the three options are viewed side by side.

**Usage:** Page titles, section headings (H1, H2), hero statements, pull quotes. Set with generous letter-spacing (+0.02em at H1, +0.01em at H2) and ample line-height (1.2) to reinforce the elevated, spacious feel.

### Body Typeface: DM Sans

**Source:** Google Fonts (free)
**Classification:** Geometric sans-serif with humanist warmth

DM Sans provides clean, highly readable body text with a geometric structure that signals modernity. Its slightly rounded terminals and open apertures maintain approachability, preventing the type system from feeling cold or clinical. The contrast between Instrument Serif's editorial sharpness at display sizes and DM Sans's friendly geometry at body sizes creates a dynamic tension — sophisticated headings grounded by accessible body text. At body sizes (16-18px), DM Sans feels effortless to read without becoming invisible or generic.

**Usage:** Body copy, navigation, buttons, captions, metadata. Set at 16px minimum (18px preferred for primary content), line-height 1.6, with relaxed paragraph spacing (1.5em). Button text in DM Sans Medium (500 weight), all-caps with wide letter-spacing (+0.08em) for CTAs. **CTA button text color:** Use Warm Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) reversed on Terracotta Rose (`#C4887B`) button backgrounds for large text (18px+ bold), or Charcoal Umber (`#3B3230`) on Terracotta Rose for body-size text (below 18px) to ensure WCAG AA compliance.

### Pairing Rationale

This pairing balances editorial sophistication with everyday approachability through deliberate contrast. Instrument Serif's sharp, high-contrast display forms establish the brand's elevated positioning at headline scale, while DM Sans's geometric warmth ensures the reading experience remains friendly and accessible. The serif-to-sans contrast creates unmistakable visual hierarchy. The system avoids the common wellness trap of pairing a decorative script with a thin sans-serif — instead, both faces are structurally grounded and contemporary. The generous spacing and sizing specifications ensure the typography breathes, supporting the elevated-but-warm positioning.

## Logo Concept

### Form: Geometric Marigold Rosette

The logo is a simplified, geometric rosette inspired by the overhead view of a marigold flower in full bloom. The form is constructed from 9 overlapping rounded petal shapes arranged radially — each rotated 40 degrees from the last (9 petals x 40 degrees = 360 degrees) — creating precise mandala-like symmetry. Each petal is a single, uniform shape (a tapered ellipse with rounded ends), rotated at equal intervals around a shared center point.

### Construction Details

- **Geometry:** The petals radiate from a small central circle (representing the flower's disc). Each petal overlaps its neighbors by approximately 20%, creating subtle intersection zones. The overall silhouette is circular.
- **Style:** Flat, single-weight line work or solid fill — no gradients, no hand-drawn texture, no illustrated detail. The form should feel like it was constructed with a compass and protractor, not drawn by hand.
- **Proportions:** The rosette sits within a perfect circle bounding box. Petal length is approximately 40% of the overall diameter. The central disc is 15% of the overall diameter.

### Wordmark

"MARIGOLD" is set in Instrument Serif, all capitals, with wide letter-spacing (+0.12em). Positioned below the rosette mark with clear vertical separation (equal to the height of one capital letter). "WELLNESS STUDIO" appears beneath in DM Sans, all capitals, smaller size (approximately 50% of "MARIGOLD" size), with matching letter-spacing.

### Lockup Variations

1. **Primary (stacked):** Rosette above, "MARIGOLD" below, "WELLNESS STUDIO" beneath that. Used when space allows vertical composition.
2. **Horizontal:** Rosette to the left, text block to the right (both lines left-aligned to each other). Used in navigation bars and horizontal spaces.
3. **Mark only:** Rosette alone. Used as favicon, social media avatar, and watermark.

### Color Application

- Primary usage: Charcoal Umber (`#3B3230`) rosette and text on Warm Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) background
- Reversed: Warm Ivory rosette and text on Charcoal Umber background
- Accent: Terracotta Rose (`#C4887B`) rosette with Charcoal Umber text — reserved for hero moments and brand collateral
- The Burnished Gold (`#C9A85C`) may be used for the central disc detail only, as a subtle brand flourish

### Symbolism

The geometric rosette connects directly to the marigold flower while abstracting it into a form that reads as contemporary and elevated. The mandala-like radial symmetry evokes balance, centering, and wholeness — core wellness concepts — without resorting to literal yoga or meditation symbols. The circular form suggests community and completeness. The geometric construction method (rather than organic illustration) positions the studio as refined and intentional.

## Imagery Direction

### Photography Style

**Mood:** Quiet confidence. Images should feel still, composed, and intentional — as if each frame was carefully considered rather than captured candidly. The overall impression is editorial wellness: aspirational but attainable, polished but not sterile.

**Lighting:** Prioritize natural light — soft, directional, with visible warmth (late morning or golden hour quality). Avoid flash-lit or overhead fluorescent looks. Light should create gentle shadows and depth, not flatten the scene. Slightly warm white balance throughout.

**Color Treatment:** Desaturated but warm. Apply a subtle grade that pulls colors toward the brand palette — muted, tonal, with lowered contrast. Blacks should be lifted slightly (never pure black in photography). Highlights should be warm, not blown. The overall treatment should feel like the photographs exist in the same world as the color palette: restrained, curated, warm.

**Subjects:**
- Studio interiors: clean spaces with natural materials (wood, linen, ceramic), minimal props, evidence of use (a rolled mat, a folded blanket) rather than pristine staging
- People in practice: mid-movement, eyes closed or gaze averted (not looking at camera), authentic body types and ages reflecting the 30-55 demographic, visible effort and ease rather than performance
- Detail shots: hands on a mat, light through a window, texture of a bolster, steam from a cup — tactile, grounding moments
- Outdoor periphery: the studio entrance, the Millbrook streetscape, natural elements (stone, plants, sky) that situate the brand in place

**Composition:** Clean and spacious. Favor wide framing with generous negative space. Use the rule of thirds loosely. Avoid tight crops on faces or busy multi-subject compositions. Every image should have a clear focal point and visual breathing room around it.

### Stock Imagery Specifications

When sourcing stock photography, prioritize:
- Natural light, indoor or outdoor settings
- Subjects aged 30-55, diverse, in relaxed or focused states (not performing for the camera)
- Muted, warm color grading — reject anything with high saturation, cool tones, or clinical lighting
- Minimal, uncluttered backgrounds
- Real yoga/Pilates/wellness settings — not white-walled photo studios

### What to Avoid

- **Women in white rooms smiling at cameras** — Sara's explicit anti-pattern. No posed, stock-standard wellness imagery.
- **High-saturation or neon accent colors** in photography — clashes with the muted palette
- **Overhead or flat-lay compositions** as a primary style — too trendy, too Instagram
- **Extreme close-ups of faces** — feels clinical or cosmetic, not wellness
- **Dark, moody, or dramatically lit imagery** — wrong energy; the studio is warm and grounding, not brooding
- **Generic beige/sage lifestyle imagery** — the second anti-pattern; imagery must feel specific and intentional, not like a Canva template
- **Busy or cluttered scenes** — Sara flagged no busy layouts; imagery should match that principle
- **Athletic or fitness-focused imagery** — this audience is health-conscious but not hardcore; avoid six-pack abs, intense sweat, or competitive energy

### Illustration Style (If Used)

If supplementary illustrations are needed (icons, decorative elements), they should be:
- Line-based, single-weight, geometric
- Drawn in Charcoal Umber or Terracotta Rose
- Minimal and structural — not organic, hand-drawn, or whimsical
- Consistent with the geometric language of the logo rosette
